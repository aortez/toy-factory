## Evaluation and explanations

Report all 16 paired five-component comparisons, both per-schedule six-component
aggregates, overall aggregate, paired win/tie/loss counts and renewal-time deltas.
For sensitivity, remove each **world seed together with both schedules**, rather
than treating its two schedules as independent replicas. Report all eight such
aggregates. No p-value or statistical-generalization claim from this small panel.

For every trial, partition the exact rewarded renewal ticks by confirmation
time: (158,166], (166,174], (174,182], (182,190]. Each eligible child has an exact
one-day confirmation, available ticks from confirmation to day 190, credited live
ticks and subsequent natural/patch death or survival through day 190. Sum these
contributions back to the unmodified C/Python score. Keep failed first-day
confirmations, uncredited founder/young-parent offspring, older descendant
occupancy and late unconfirmed births separate. Do not match numeric lineage IDs
across controllers: their histories diverge. Outcomes describe where the score
changes, not causal attribution to water/light without further measurements.

Also report the complete seed-purchase cohort (158,190], followed to day 192:
purchases, germination, expiry, child first-day survival/failure/censoring, with
explicit denominators. Preserve species/founder-family counts as diagnostics,
not extra selection terms. Reanalyze the prior two review pairs' saved ledgers
with these same explanations, labeled historical and excluded from the new panel.
