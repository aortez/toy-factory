## Intervention contract

After the ordinary guard permits the eligible winning growth proposal, inspect
only proposals that append one node. Project that proposed fixed body from
energy 256, stress zero and canonical phase 117 using the existing exact dark
reference: 149 zero-income steps / 37 upkeep payments, adequate water and no
further spending. Refuse if it dies before possible income returns. The current
mechanics imply 64 → 65 is the boundary; do not tune that boundary from results.

FINISH, blocked EXTEND, WAIT, renewal and seeds are outside the new check.
The ordinary guard retains its receipts, counters and semantics. New receipts
must identify the permitted original growth expense and full-night projection
separately; a later capacity refusal must not masquerade as a paid purchase.
Refusal commits no payment, node/tip change, controller memory/RNG, growth phase
or decision telemetry. No alternative bid is selected automatically. Retrying
and possible action starvation are outcomes to measure, not reasons to add
another mechanism during this comparison. Invalid/overflowed diagnostics fail
closed with an error. All new state is bounded, diagnostic and host-only.

## Outcomes and stopping rule

For each pair, report target fate and full-night follow-up; all natural/patch
deaths and causes; births, eligible/full-day offspring survivors, durable
descendant parents, seed production, living exposure, final living/bank species
and whole/closing windows. Do not match later newborn IDs across divergent arms.

Record every newly refused attempt, per-lineage totals and first/last timing,
longest consecutive refusal streak, later committed EXTEND/FINISH/WAIT/renewal
and seed activity, and whether plants remain at the boundary. Report repeated
receipts separately from affected plants; node indices are packed-array
locations rather than permanent physical-tip IDs. Keep failures and negative
controls, not only rescues or attractive images.

Stop after these captures and their verification. Publish evidence, update
issue 30/roadmap, and discuss the result before broader testing or adoption.
No firmware/default change, score change, automatic commit or push.
