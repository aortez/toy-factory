# Frozen dark guard: four-world, 64-day paired panel

Follow the [eight-day guard comparison](renewal-dark-guard.md) with one bounded
longer panel. Keep `dark-spending-guard-v1`, native binaries, model routing and
all resource/ecology rules unchanged. This is exploratory existing-review
evidence, not held-out qualification or training. Fix this protocol before
collection; do not select different worlds, horizons or thresholds from results.

## Inputs and panel

Use the completed `artifacts/garden-renewal-dark-guard-v1` bundle, manifest
SHA-256 `0078077eeabf0fe95393870a626ebb8f9c407d62d7f6225d5dce8f89fcbaaa52`.
Copy its control/guard inspector and replay executables byte-for-byte, together
with both models, build metadata and source archive. Verify the existing bundle
and require current native sources to match its source fingerprints. No rebuild,
C edit, new seed-reserve rule, controller mutation or model search is needed.

- Four worlds: the complete earlier replication review set, in declared order:
  `abf7af73`, `58e36558`, `0d983a80`, `beda710e`. Do not replace the selected
  `0d983a80` failure example or omit adverse companions.
- Two conditions: **no patches**, and existing `patch-death-v1` schedule seed
  `05d87ca0`, first event on day 16. Schedule coordinates/times are independent
  of population; victims may differ between arms. One schedule does not test
  robustness to other schedules.
- Two arms: ordinary **control**, and **guard**, with no focal quota or other
  reserve gate. Eight matched world/condition pairs, sixteen trajectories.
- Same rainfed-crowded layout, no gardener, wide dispersal, headroom water
  uptake, selective leaf renewal, no-night-growth policy, 512 nodes and eight
  plant/seed slots. No drainage. Background and descendants use N `01b9d94a`;
  founder 5 alone uses W `c9ea07fd`. This is not an all-N/all-W policy comparison.
- Horizon **245,760 logic ticks / 64 garden days**, below the existing 256-day
  age-wrap boundary. Closing window: **days 48–64**. Events are counted in
  `(start, stop]`; time exposure integrates post-event states on `[start, stop)`.

## Execution budget and images

Capture every ecology step, all ordinary bids and all guard candidates, twice
per trajectory: **32 native inspector calls**. Preserve raw traces; explicitly
validate pre-event / patch / post-event triples without inventing or erasing
natural deaths. Repeats must agree byte-for-byte.

Capture every trajectory at days **8, 32 and 64**, twice per frame:
**96 native replay calls**, **128 experimental native calls total**. No extra
diagnostic worlds, parameter trials or automatic reruns. Unit/regression tests
are separate. Show every case, including empty worlds; the eight comparison rows
are in fixed world/condition order, with control/guard paired at each checkpoint.

The selected world's first eight days must match the old complete traces in
both arms and conditions. For each world/arm the two conditions must match
through strictly before the first patch, including the pre-patch step. Each
frame must match its own trace state and framebuffer CRC, and its exact repeat.

## Measures and checks

Keep raw candidates. Generalize only offline accounting interfaces where needed
to accept a declared horizon and validated patch boundaries. Remove only rejected,
uncommitted growth bids from a derived accounting stream; keep original census
states and leaf proposals. Verify every native pre/post forecast independently,
candidate ordering/denials and cumulative counters. Reconcile live resource,
leaf-condition, growth-decision, seed-production and birth/death accounting.
Natural terminal steps keep their cleared-budget limitation; patch losses are
separately known from the event records.

Per case, preserve complete lifetime records and daily population checkpoints.
Report whole and closing-window births, natural versus environmental deaths,
seed production/expiration, seed production by descendants, founder/species/
family retention, maximum generation, extinction and living/tipless/descendant
exposure. Track capacity occupancy without attributing failed recruitment to a
particular seed-site condition that was not observed directly.

Report all offspring and closing-born offspring separately, with full-day
follow-up eligibility, survivors, recent alive/dead and durable offspring
parents (the parent and at least one child each survive a full garden day).
Also count descendant parents that actually buy seeds in the closing window;
neither a full seed bank nor mature founder survival substitutes for this.

Summarize paired differences separately for no-patch and patch conditions,
show every seed's direction and retain adverse cases. Repeats, shared prefixes
and two conditions on one world are not independent replicates. No new fitness
scalar, fitted acceptance threshold or retrospective model selection.

Freeze sources, binaries, protocol, models and commands before collection;
preserve all artifacts and fingerprints. Analysis must repeat exactly and work
offline. Keep a portable JSON summary, all fixed native PNGs and a labeled
gallery in the repo, with the complete frozen bundle ignored locally. Update
issue 30 and the roadmap with results and caveats. Discuss the next proposal
before further scope expansion, promotion or training. No commit, push or
device deployment is authorized by this experiment.
