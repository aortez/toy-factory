   costs for each seedling. Retain the death checkpoint but never infer its
   cleared income or resource debit. Separate paid upkeep from unpaid demand.
2. Compare birth phase, first possible global income, actual first income and
   zero-income intervals. A bright sky is not evidence that a leaf is lit.
3. Recover exact pre-storage photosynthesis numerator as
   `income * 255 + new_remainder - old_remainder`. Check integer bounds and
   accounting. For non-newborn live steps all previous leaves have matured
   during the fifteen intervening logic ticks; verify this against the sampled
   mature-leaf count. Account for pre-uptake wear. With positive condition on
   every such leaf, a zero numerator proves all their light bands are zero.
   Do not infer individual light values or a specific shading neighbor.
4. Join actual committed growth to recorded winning bids/agent counters.
   Report growth before the first income, root/shoot choice and recorded
   available shoot-candidate light. Refused and uncommitted bids are not
