controller, dusk policy, training run, or firmware change.

## Collection and checks

Freeze sources, protocol, models, build flags/graphs, binaries and commands
before native collection. Six full ecology traces (three arms, each repeated),
all to 245760 ticks / 64 garden days. Eighteen frame replays (three arms, each
repeated at ticks 72960, 122880, 245760 = days 19, 32, 64): 24 native calls total.
Native unit tests/invalid-CLI tests and non-collecting builds are not experiment
runs. No exploratory shorter simulations, training, extra seeds, or sweeps.
Failed captures are retained, never silently overwritten/retried.

The disabled-hook control must reproduce the frozen raw trace byte for byte.
Each intervention must reproduce every raw bid, census/hash, patch boundary
and guard event before its selected transaction (ignoring only new veto
metadata); matching bids at the transaction must also be retained. Require
exactly one eligible veto and the expected immediate cost/body/tip delta.
Require identical repeated traces and repeated frame metadata/pixels. Native
replay hashes must equal their own trace census; render must not mutate state.
Reconcile all live energy/water budgets, guard forecasts/counters, actual paid
actions, leaf mass, stress, tips, births, deaths, and patch losses independently.

## Outcomes, not just target rescue

Follow each target through night and dawn, later purchases/retries, reproduction,
and death/censoring. Record all original lineages and newly created descendants,
natural versus imposed patch deaths, whole-world population/species/families,
seed/birth flows, full-day offspring survival, durable parent-child pairs,
tipless and living exposure, and the established days 48–64 closing window.
Compare identities only when shared ancestry/history justifies it: IDs assigned
after divergence need not represent the same plant. Keep adverse neighbor
outcomes and censoring, not just successful target examples.

Inspect the nine native frames in a fixed gallery. One selected adverse world
and two post-hoc-selected purchases cannot establish broad policy efficacy.
No general guard change follows automatically from either outcome.
