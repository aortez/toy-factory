trace schema have already been inspected; this is explanatory reuse of one
selected world, not a blinded test or independent validation.

## Questions and required accounting

1. Partition natural and patch deaths by founders, offspring born before the
   intervention, and offspring born after it. Reconcile the whole-world death
   delta without pairing coincident newborn IDs after divergence.
2. Retain every post-intervention offspring's parent, species, location, birth
   and death/censoring. Report full-day eligible/surviving/recent cohorts by
   species and column; include the same day-48–64 window. Extra births are not
   themselves better survival, and proportions in this selected world are not
   independent-trial estimates.
3. Reconstruct seed purchases and outcomes only where ordered bank transitions,
   fixed dormancy/lifetime, new-child ancestry and creation/expiry counters
   determine them. Seeds have no logged ID/age: derive identity from parent
   and creation tick, retaining the bank's append/remove order. Reject ambiguous
   or inconsistent transitions, rather than invent an identity. Reconcile
   per-parent seed production, every child and endpoint bank. Disturbances
   preserve seeds; ordinary and post-patch samples are not separate steps.
4. Follow all shrub seeds, particularly the surviving pre-intervention shrub
   6: purchases, germination/expiry/pending, landing columns, timing, final
   living/bank disappearance, and observed blocker combinations. Distinguish
   seeds already pending at intervention from subsequent purchases.
5. Seed blockers in these traces are **post-step observations**, not the
   pre-germination decision receipts. Do not infer an unobserved open site,
   exact shading cause, alternative landing, or successful counterfactual
   from them. Track observed spacing occupants (including unreclaimed dead
   plants), plant capacity, light, moisture and node capacity separately.
6. For every post-intervention natural death, preserve birth/last-live state,
   first shortage, exact live resource totals and dark-entry forecast where
   supported. Do not reconstruct cleared terminal budgets. Compare timing,
   upkeep, growth/FINISH costs and observed income without claiming that a
   particular untested refusal would rescue that child.
