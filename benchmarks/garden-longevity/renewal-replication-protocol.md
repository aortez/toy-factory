Replicate the objective comparison, not the previous selected controller. Start
every search from original `dc5e849d`. The preceding A/B remains separate,
uncommitted evidence; its review panel must not select or tune this experiment.
No score, ecology, model interface, firmware, search depth or mutation-rate change.

## Unchanged search, objectives and budget

Three generations of three offspring per arm; 32 native parameter mutations per
offspring; ten evaluated candidates including original. Siblings use the
generation-start parent; strictly better replaces it, exact ties retain incumbent
then first canonical better child. Keep all candidates, failures and unchanged
champions. Do not select generation two retrospectively using review.

Run every world to day 192 (737280 logic ticks). A uses the native (158,190]
window with two-day follow-up. B reuses four periods (62,94], (94,126], (126,158],
(158,190], each with its own two-day follow-up and 32-day post-confirmation living
credit cap. Its lexicographic key remains minimum terminal tier, sum of tiers,
sum of each world's minimum-period credit, total credit. No hard zero-gap gate,
diversity bonus or opportunity-normalized denominator. The whole selector is the
treatment. Both score views are reported; only the declared training key selects.

For **each replica**, retain the preceding pilot budget:

- 320 training + 320 full capture-disabled repeat trials.
- 64 review ledger trials (two arms × four generations × eight conditions).
- 128 image replays, independently repeating all 64 day-192 captures.
- 36 native mutations across both arms and their full repeats.

Across R1/R2: **1,408 ledger trials + 256 replays + 72 mutations = 1,736 native
processes**. There are 128 required PNGs. Complete replicas may run concurrently
in separate directories with immutable inputs, at most two jobs. Each search and
its native worlds remain serial; this is not a new general parallel trainer.
No further worlds, mutation streams, generations, challenge runs or outcome-based
budget extension. Verification reuses saved records, without native execution.

## Verification, reporting and stop

Freeze source/input hashes, binaries, configuration, seeds, selectors and native
command logs. Full capture-disabled repeats must reproduce all model bytes,
native ledgers, RNG ancestry, scores and champion histories. Original training
and review worlds must agree across all four searches; common first-generation
mutants must agree within each replica. Verify every C/Python legacy score,
bounded-period sample oracle, additive credit identity, saved native frame and
independent repeated pixels. Test immutable custom profiles, train/review routing,
RNG isolation, mixed-profile rejection and unchanged defaults. Reverify prior
mixed/coverage/A-B bundles. Retain partial evidence if collection fails; do not
silently restart or publish a complete manifest on failure.

Primary: **final B versus final A under the bounded key, separately for R1 and
R2**, on the fixed fresh review panel. Mandatory controls are each final versus
original. Report both score views, every generation's training/review, every
paired world, both schedule groups, blocked world-seed omissions, and diversity
and cohort outcomes. A joint summary must retain replica labels and account for
shared worlds/original controls; no pseudo-replicated confidence claim. Survival
precedence cannot be hidden behind a credit percentage. One favorable replica
does not establish a repeatable objective advantage.

Export one eight-row/eight-column native gallery per replica (A0..A3, B0..B3),
full paired score/cohort diagnostics and all 128 PNGs, including failures. Record
findings in the roadmap and issue #30. Discuss any next step; no automatic
objective adoption, model promotion, firmware deployment, extra training, commit,
PR or push. Existing uncommitted work must be preserved.
