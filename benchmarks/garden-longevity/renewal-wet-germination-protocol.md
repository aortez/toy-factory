## Outcomes and decision

Primary: post-activation births, full-day eligible offspring and survivors,
durable descendant parents and later descendant reproduction. Also follow
rescued shrub 7's seeds/offspring, plus whichever competing family occupies the
opening; a different winner is a result, not grounds to retarget the experiment.

Report every post-activation child's first-day shortages, deaths, later seed
production and offspring. Keep recent living seedlings/seeds censored at the
horizon. Separate natural deaths from the fixed export, and pre-existing bank
seeds from new purchases. Include whole/post/late plant and seed-slot occupancy,
full-slot time, species/founder-family retention, living exposure and costs.
More germinations with worse survival or more blocked slots is not a success;
one long-lived founder child is not evidence of generational renewal.

Repeat analysis and verify portable results/images. Run default and experimental
host suites, explicit disabled/firmware guards and focused gate/state/boundary
tests. Record the result and next proposal in issue #30 and the roadmap. Stop
uncommitted: no push, device deployment, training, broader panel, parameter
retuning or automatic promotion is included.
