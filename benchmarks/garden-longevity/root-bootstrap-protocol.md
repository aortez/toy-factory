# Predeclared wet-root bootstrap experiment

After the read-only seedling audit, unchanged native bid replays cover
c7f54e18/reserve/16, b61837dc/baseline/8 and /16, and b61837dc/reserve/16,
all with the seed-reserve gate on. All 49,153 ordinary world rows and fixed-patch
boundaries per case must equal their frozen inputs. All closing offspring's
first-day bids are retained, including survivors and rooted failures.

Those observations confirm wet, unblocked, affordable root EXTEND bids lose to
shoots in all seven targeted water failures. No intervention outcomes have been
collected when this protocol is written.

## Fixed intervention

`--root-bootstrap-after 614400` enables `wet-root-bootstrap-v1` only in the host
inspector/replayer. All eight **gate-on** worlds from the previous seed-reserve
panel are paired with probe-disabled controls: two world seeds, baseline
no-night-growth/reserve-growth policies, eight/sixteen seed banks. The model,
512 nodes, leaf maintenance, rain, patch schedule, capacities and prices remain
unchanged. Horizon is day 192; activation follows day 160. All earlier world
states must match exactly after removing the new identity metadata.

Eligible offspring are born strictly after the activation tick, no older than
256 ecology-age ticks, with exactly two root nodes. The candidate tip is a
depth-one root in the surface soil row. The base policy must already propose
EXTEND: night/energy WAIT and FINISH are never overridden. Stores must cover
ordinary energy and water growth prices.

Among the base proposal's available candidates in a deeper soil row, choose the
wettest with moisture strictly above the current tip cell and at least the
species' growth water cost (five, five, four). Ties retain base candidate order.
Move that candidate first and set the bid priority to INT16_MAX; other candidates
retain relative order. Inter-tip ties retain the engine's ordinary first-bid
rule. No resource gift, new memory field or special growth operation is added.
After one successful root extension the plant has three roots and is no longer
eligible. Founders and pre-window offspring remain unchanged.

This is a diagnostic intervention, not an adaptive gardener, trained model or
default policy. Current local observations select the action; world identity
and time only select the experimental cohort. No future weather/soil lookup,
lineage-specific rescue or outcome-dependent change is permitted.

## Measurements and checks

- Fresh probe-off native replays must reproduce all eight frozen gate-on
  histories, including exact terminal and patch-boundary records.
- Independently reconstruct every offered override from the base proposal and
  local candidate telemetry. Audit committed winners separately from losing
  bids; verify at most one successful override per lineage and ordinary costs.
- Require identical pre-intervention prefixes. Record first changed winning bid
  and first authoritative hash difference separately; telemetry alone is not
  necessarily an authoritative world change.
- Full live resource ledgers, first-day water/root histories, birth/death flags,
  occupancy, seeds, and age 1/2/4/8/16 survival with explicit censoring.
- Retain energy deaths and later failures: reducing early thirst is not enough
  if it merely shifts mortality into the first night or creates larger adults.
- Render day 192 natively in all probe-on worlds; match replay hashes to traces
  and compare with the frozen off frames. Retain all pairs, not just successes.
- Keep rule/threshold/horizon fixed. No extra hyperparameter search or training.

New descendants need not correspond by ID across arms after divergence. The
closing cohort and longer-age eligible cohorts, not cherry-picked surviving
lineages, drive the descriptive comparison. This repeatedly inspected small
panel cannot establish held-out performance or indefinite persistence.
