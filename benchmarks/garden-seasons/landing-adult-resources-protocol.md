# Adult resources and generation overlap protocol

This read-only investigation follows the saved turnover diagnosis. It explains
the resource history of older plants and concurrent germination blockers in one
already-selected seasonal pair. It does not change biology, policy, scoring or
device firmware, and is not a generalization test or a rescue experiment.

## Fixed inputs and scope

Use the frozen single-seed current-input controller `single-s6d697834-live` and
the `rainfed-crowded-seasonal-940cfdc4` condition from the arbitration archive.
Retain both `veto` and `eligible` arms, with ordinary ecology and gardener off.
Verify input archive SHA-256
`7e3a88e1077f0944b84c412675a6bfabc1b531da6bf21844f62be68ac7b4bec7`.
Continue each unchanged replay to day 192 and compare its complete result to the
saved history, not just an endpoint count.

Capture all living plants, new births, and same-step deaths on every ecology
sample from day 96 through day 130 inclusive. This includes births of the known
eligible cohort 25–28 around day 104.85, its loss around day 126.6, and recovery
of established descendants around day 128.85. Plants already alive at the start
have left-censored resource histories; do not describe their captured budgets
as lifetime totals. Older corpses are excluded, with lineage IDs used instead
of compacted plant slots. Capture every seed attempt in that same window.

## Required receipts and checks

Reuse host-only uptake and stage receipts: energy and water before uptake,
actual income and overflow, stores after uptake, upkeep, germination, growth
and reproduction, and terminal resource discard. Reconcile every plant-step
and accumulated window account. Report stress and shortage flags; count upkeep
shortages only on maintenance steps, not the intervening retained flags.
Record daily sunlight phase/strength, seasonal state and actual unsaturated
soil totals. Morphology counts are post-step diagnostics, not measurements of
the leaves that collected energy, especially on a death step.

Check the full plant and seed inventory against the independent lifetime/seed
ledger, including births, deaths, seed expiry and in-window partial lifetimes.
Missing, duplicate, reordered, malformed or unbalanced records must fail.
Existing first-day trace bytes must remain unchanged.

Run traced and untraced replay pairs and repeat the trace. Require equal complete
results and framebuffers, byte-identical repeated traces, unchanged model bytes,
and exact historical day-192 results/pixels. Capture both arms at days 120, 124,
126.5, 127, 128, 129 and 130 with the shared production renderer. Images are
selected diagnostic examples, not unseen evaluation evidence.

## Stopping point

Report where adult reserves are lost, what spending precedes shortages, and
which seed blockers coexist before and during the gap. Separate measured death
shortages from hypotheses about the controller or environmental causes.
Conclude with one justified next intervention or the exact remaining unknown.
Do not retrain, relax score gates, add energy guards, widen dispersal or alter
germination timing in this investigation. Keep existing whole-panel retention
failures and counterexamples intact.
