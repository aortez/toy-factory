# Dawn reserve spending comparison protocol

Test whether the dawn reserve proposed in the saved funding audit prevents the
remaining morning shortages without losing useful growth or any of the five
survivors. This is a fixed diagnostic panel, not training or population qualification.

## One treatment, unchanged controls

Control is the previous daylight candidate. Both arms keep the same birth-time
root purchase, continuous protection of only the named living nonfounder from
age two, water guard, afternoon maintenance-increase condition and productive
daylight credit. No new protected plants or descendants, prices, priorities,
destinations, memory, RNG, capacity, resource grants or ecology changes.

For paid actions at phases 64..127, let B(q) be the unscaled sun curve minus
ambient 24 and C be current observed strength minus 24. Find the first q in
1..64 with `24 + floor(B(q) * C / B(now)) >= 64`. Round q up to a multiple of
four and reserve that many maintenance payments using post-action upkeep.
Require corrected projected sunset minus nominal night upkeep minus this
reserve to be nonnegative. For maintenance-increasing actions, also subtract
the reserve from the existing stores-only margin. Keep all old checks conjoined.

If no productive morning is estimated, the paid afternoon bid is ineligible;
there is no invented buffer or access to future weather, income or bodies.
Morning, night, WAIT, birth purchases and nonfocal behavior remain unchanged.
Add explicit host-only `--root-dawn-reserve`, requiring `--root-daylight-energy`
and its prerequisites, a GLM1 identity, and `--stand-dawn` observational receipts.
Receipts distinguish applicability, unavailable estimates, independently allowed
and active states, first phase, payment phase, reserve and both margins.
Unconfigured hashes and old schemas must remain unchanged.
