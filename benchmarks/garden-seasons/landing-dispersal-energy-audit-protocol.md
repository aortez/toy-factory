# Audit energy shortfalls after broader late dispersal

Status: **FROZEN before computing new audit outcomes**. The completed
[late dispersal comparison](landing-late-dispersal.md) improves establishment
but worsens descendant energy shortfalls per maintenance payment in ten of
sixteen comparisons. Determine which plants and exposures account for that
change before proposing another ecology rule.

Perform no native simulations, training, device operations, rule changes or

uses saved summaries and body histories, not a new raw-trace extraction.
Stop if provenance, coverage or accounting fails.

## Separate cohorts and exposure

Keep founders separate from the descendant harm screen. For descendants,
partition whole lifetimes retrospectively into those dead by intervention,
those alive at intervention, and five exhaustive later-birth groups: died by
one day, survived one day but died by 32 days, confirmed 32-day survival,
one-day follow-up censored, and one-day survival confirmed with 32-day follow-up
censored. Use the existing exact-checkpoint death and censoring conventions.
Also report whether each plant is alive at the horizon and has reproduced.

Use logic-tick windows `(0,983040]`, `(0,626580]`, `(626580,983040]`
and `(860160,983040]` for whole, pre-intervention, post-intervention and final
32-day maintenance accounts. Newborns pay no maintenance on their birth step;
death-step maintenance is included. Report unpaid units, shortage events,
maintenance payments and total units due for both energy and water. Preserve
zero-exposure rates as unavailable, not zero or a pass.

Match births using recursive parent identity, birth tick, origin column and
species, never numeric lineage ID alone. Split descendant whole-life totals
into shared pre-intervention history, prior occupants' common post-intervention
exposure, their unequal survival tails, matched later recruits' common exposure,
their unequal tails, and unmatched later recruits. The common endpoint is the
earlier death or observation horizon. Require the same number of maintenance
payments on each common interval, but report differing body costs rather than
assuming equal demand. Assert that the pre-intervention accounts agree.

Reconcile this partition with the original screen and the cohort partition.
Use exact rational rate differences and additive group contributions with
each arm's original total-payment denominator. Contributions are accounting,
not causal effects. Also report each common group's own equal-exposure rate
and its body-demand difference so changed denominators cannot hide individual
deterioration.

## Check timing and body costs

Reconstruct maintenance demand from the last recorded living body strictly
before each payment: energy costs `ceil(nodes/8)` and water costs
`ceil((nodes-roots)/8)`. Growth on a maintenance step is paid only from the
next maintenance step. Check reconstructed shapes at every saved shortage
and require lifetime demand to equal paid upkeep plus unpaid units. A mismatch
invalidates the audit rather than being silently approximated.

Retain every post-intervention energy-shortfall event with plant identity,
cohort, exact sun phase, age, pre-maintenance nodes and roots, both resources'
due/paid/unpaid values and whether it is the death step. Summarize by cohort,
sun-phase quarter, age below one day / one to 32 days / at least 32 days,
node count and water-shortfall concurrence. These are repeated events, not
independent samples. Phase quarters are descriptive clock bins, not inferred
leaf illumination or season labels.

## Deliverable and decision boundary

Save a compact deterministic result, offline reanalysis/verification command,
boundary and tamper tests, and a report covering all sixteen comparisons.
Include original whole-life and post-intervention rates and an exhaustive
cohort decomposition. Verify a second export is byte-identical, and forbid
subprocess launching during audit tests and verification.

The result can identify who incurs shortfalls, exposure and demand changes,
timing, survival and seed production. It cannot on its own establish shading,
rain, resource competition or seed spending as the cause of an individual
deficit; lifetime income totals are not equal-window transfer histories.
If causal diagnosis needs those histories, propose a focused read of saved
adult receipts next. Do not relax the harm screen or promote broader dispersal
on the basis of this audit.
