# Founder construction and reproduction trace protocol

Explain why capped seasonal flower founders buy no seeds, without changing
their policy. Compare the same twelve frozen model/world pairs and off/32
arms as the construction screen. Include the two capped steady flower
founders that do reproduce, not just the seasonal failures.

The input is `landing-growth-results.json.gz`, SHA-256
`9027651cd3da4d8a9a8703ef7a78d4ecc351faef35ece5fe4f2ee43f5605d2da`.
No new search, controller selection, intervention or ecology change is allowed.

## Fixed collection

Replay each arm to day 192. Record founder construction proposals and exact
sequential reproduction visits on every ecology tick from tick 15 through
day 64 inclusive. This covers every capped seasonal flower's complete living
history (all die around day 56), and every successful flower founder's first
seed purchase (all before day one). Steady survivors and the long-lived
uncapped seasonal founder remain right-censored in the trace window, although
their complete seed and lifetime ledgers remain available through day 192.

Record every founder tip query before and after the existing cap projection,
including tissue, depth, flowering threshold, node/root/shoot counts, resources,
available destinations, action and priority. These are proposals before water
eligibility and arbitration, not selected/executed actions. Include living
founder post-step bodies and active tips. Reuse the authoritative reproduction
audit's simultaneous blockers, flower categories, reserves and purchase flag.
Do not evaluate an alternate policy or consume an extra random draw.

Also record the existing full adult resource trace from tick 15 through day
two. Reconcile its budgets, founder deaths and purchases with the new observer
and the full historical ledgers. This early resource window is not evidence
about later founder resource transfers.

Each arm must match its archived initial/final result and endpoint pixels,
an untraced run, and an independently repeated traced run. Both raw traces
must repeat byte-for-byte. Preserve frozen models, binary, build settings,
protocol, sources, traces and artifact hashes in a fresh ignored bundle.
Publish the portable receipts and summaries only after verification succeeds.

## Questions and limits

For every founder, distinguish no flower nodes, immature/spent flowers, and
ready flowers blocked by other gates. Count simultaneous gates as overlapping
restrictions, not independent causes. Identify times when missing flowers are
the only reproduction blocker. Relate these to the raw/applied growth bids
and the actual post-step structure, without calling every proposal a winner.

Check whether all halted stems are below flowering depth, or whether mature
enough tips simply never FINISH. Retain counterexamples by model, climate and
species. Matched founders may be compared across arms; descendant IDs must
not be paired after the trajectories diverge.

No rescue experiment, forced FINISH, larger cap, retraining or device flash
follows automatically. Finish with the observed mechanism and a narrowly
motivated next proposal. Full-panel renewal retention remains unchanged.
