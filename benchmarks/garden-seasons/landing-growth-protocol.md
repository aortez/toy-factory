# Maintenance aware construction protocol

Test whether limiting construction to 32 nodes improves adult energy survival
and generation overlap without losing renewal elsewhere. This is one host-only
policy experiment, not a device change or a training run. The earlier adult
resource trace found 36-node plants paying five energy units per upkeep; 32 nodes
would pay four, but could also collect fewer resources.

## Fixed treatment and controls

Use the six frozen single and multi-seed controllers and all twelve steady and
seasonal conditions from the arbitration panel. Keep model bytes, ordinary
ecology, eight seed slots, weather, placement, storage, gardener-off reset,
192-day horizon, and rolling renewal scoring unchanged. Verify the arbitration
archive SHA-256
`7e3a88e1077f0944b84c412675a6bfabc1b531da6bf21844f62be68ac7b4bec7`.

Both new run modes use the existing first-day water-eligible arbitration.
`off` must exactly reproduce its archived results, first-day traces, and pixels.
`32` projects an EXTEND that has an available destination to WAIT when the
observed plant already has at least 32 nodes. Apply this to founders and
descendants from construction onward. Preserve priority, chosen tip and neural
next-memory; apply the existing water eligibility and priority ranking to the
projected bids. Do not rerank against the cap or invent a FINISH action. A
blocked high-priority bid may therefore win as WAIT. This tests this explicit
projection, not every possible maintenance-aware growth policy.

Leave normal FINISH_TIP, nonallocating EXTEND cleanup, reproduction and all
resource charges unchanged. Do not remove tissue or gift resources. Give the
new run its own versioned identity including the cap setting, even when off.
Unsupported caps, missing/duplicate options, incompatible trace modes, and
nonfresh outputs must fail.

## Evidence and acceptance

Collect complete ledgers, first-day resource traces and end frames for every
pair. Require repeat result, trace and pixel equality, traced/untraced equality,
and unchanged models. Preserve the original veto and ordinary controls as
additional archived comparisons. Reuse the existing water-screen gates against
each baseline: retain the rolling score key and late child survivors in both
climates, improve a key or renewal confirmations, and introduce no earlier or
new biological emptiness. These gates do not make this reused panel unseen
validation or qualify a controller for promotion.

For the already-selected single-seed seasonal case, collect every adult/seed
receipt from days 96 through 130 and frames at 120, 124, 126.5, 127, 128, 129 and
130 for both cap settings. Reconcile uptake, overflow, spending and death
discard. Report established-descendant gaps separately from biological
extinction and compare morphology and resource income. Changed trajectories
must not be described as individually matched rescues based on lineage ID.

Freeze the binary, compiler configuration, inputs, protocol and source hashes
before collection. Save a portable result archive and checksummed full bundle.
Stop after this fixed comparison. Do not retune the cap, retrain, change storage,
relax gates or launch a different rescue based on the result.
