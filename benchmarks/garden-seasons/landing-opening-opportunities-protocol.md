# Reproductive opportunities during the second opening

This is an offline analysis plan for an already observed experiment, not a new
preregistered simulation. It asks when usable space, reproductive eligibility
and bank access coincided for every original focal plant and first recruit.
The result will guide the next proposal, not change rules automatically.

## Frozen scope

Use all five worlds and both arms of the [second-opening experiment](landing-second-opening.md).
Retain all seven original focal plants and five first recruits, including those
that never bought a seed. The worlds share one model and weather seed; this is
selected-history mechanism analysis, not an independent generalization panel.
No native simulations, training, device changes, additional deaths, increased
capacity or relaxed spacing/reserves are authorized by this analysis.

## Window and phase definitions

For each treatment, derive the interval from the recorded second victim's slot
reclamation, inclusive, to its first new germination, **exclusive**. Confirm all
interior post-step inventories have exactly seven living/stored plants. Apply
the same calendar interval to its control, whose eight-plant state is retained,
not relabeled as another opening. Include the preceding and closing observations
separately to prove the boundaries. Never extend the interval to find a success.

The simulator checks seeds before growth and reproduction. The germination that
closes the opening therefore consumes the slot before that tick's reproductive
visits. Do not count that closing tick as an available new-purchase opportunity.
Report ecology-step and daylight maintenance-cadence denominators separately.

Retain every row in each interval and every ordered visitor. For support/site
comparisons, use the recorded mature-seed site maps from that step's seed checks.
Require consistent maps across non-expired checks, no interior births, no
resource/node changes between germination and growth stages, and agreement with
post-step site maps whenever a purchase is observed. Recheck spacing, global
plant/node capacity, cold and observed seed-site light/moisture bits. Missing or
phase-incompatible observations are an error, not an invented open site.

These maps describe observed mature-seed viability in the same step. A newly
purchased seed still needs eight ecology steps of dormancy. Co-occurrence is
**not** a simulated counterfactual, guaranteed landing, future viability, or
proof that lowering a reserve would be safe.

## Dispersal evidence and uncertainty

Use actual purchase observations anywhere in the saved day-288-to-320 portable
window to identify a parent's dispersal trait. A living parent's genome does
not mutate; mutations belong to its seeds. Observations may be shared across
the paired arms only for identical pre-event lineage identities with the
validated common history. Retain every such witness and check that all agree.
Do not transfer traits between post-event numeric IDs.

For a focal/first recruit with no recorded purchase, enumerate all five legal
traits, -2 through +2. Preserve reflected/clamped choice aliases. Report each
trait separately and conservative lower/upper bounds across fixed trait values;
never switch an unknown plant's trait between ticks to manufacture an opportunity.
Label known versus bounded support. Enumerated possibilities are not probabilities
and do not reconstruct an unrecorded RNG draw.

## Accounting and outputs

For each plant, arm and legal trait, retain complete counts and relevant visit
receipts, including zeros:

- Reachable spacing-clear columns, capacity-compatible columns and columns
  passing all current mature-seed gates. Keep geometry separate from light,
  moisture, cold and global capacity.
- Daylight maintenance-cadence visits, ready flowers, protected energy/water
  margins, and eligibility ignoring only the bank. Keep all simultaneous
  blockers instead of assuming the first failed gate is the sole cause.
- Distinguish ineligible plants, eligible plants facing a bank full at entry,
  eligible plants whose opening earlier buyers filled, and actual purchases.
  Reconcile sequential bank counts, released seeds and ledger purchases.
- Count overlap of useful reachable sites with eligibility and with actual
  bank access. Report space-only and all-current-gates overlap separately;
  absence of ground light now does not forbid making a seed for later daylight.

Produce per-world/per-plant results for both cohorts, bounds and denominator
totals, a concise report, and a deterministic portable analysis artifact. Retain
the closing birth and its old/new seed identity without inventing alternatives.
Verify regeneration byte for byte with subprocess launches prohibited. Tests
must reject missing/reordered rows, changed boundaries, conflicting/guessed
traits, incompatible site maps, lost-bank misclassification and changed derived
counts. Existing source results must still verify unchanged.

The next decision must follow the observed overlap. A lack of legal purchases
is not automatically a placement failure; a legal site is not automatically
an affordable seed; a full bank is not necessarily the only failed gate.
