# Recurring winter openings and sustained renewal

Frozen before implementation and research calls. Compare four additional
non-targeted winter diebacks with the stopped schedule after the
[waiting-time comparison](landing-seed-wait.md). Keep the purchase cursor in
both arms. The question is whether recurring space supports established
parent–child renewal, not merely purchases or refilling vacancies.

## Shared history and fixed schedule

Use the six ordered cursor gardens and W2 model from
`landing-seed-wait-results.json.gz`, SHA-256
`05989040f933de126a5d4a1fba6c484608059c26da6642872cc9607d864b6dbb`.
Both arms preserve all fifteen original spatial diebacks ending day 304,
cursor activation strictly after day 320, and identical histories through
day 384. Waiting-time priority is disabled. All resource/construction gates,
seed placement, weather, fees, capacities and seed viability remain fixed.

Treatment adds four events after the ordinary step and observers:

| Garden day | Inclusive base columns |
| --- | --- |
| 396 | 4 through 7 |

| 460 | 12 through 15 |
| 492 | 16 through 19 |

These are winter starts, phase 12 of the 16-day seasonal cycle. The 32-day
interval is every other winter, four times slower than the earlier eight-day
sweep, and equal to the primary establishment horizon. It is a fixed design
choice, not a fitted optimum. The old sweep last hit columns 0 through 3;
continue at the next band without inspecting occupancy. Retain empty events.
All living plants with bases in a hit band die, regardless of age, lineage,
species, resources or controller. No fallback band or protected cohort.

Use the existing environmental-death operation. Dead bodies and their slots
remain until normal decomposition. Seeds, soil, RNG and surviving memories
are preserved; victim stores are discarded without refunds. Record imposed
deaths separately from natural deaths and physical removals. This is an
explicit host diagnostic of recurring disturbance, not a change to natural
winter mortality or the device/default ecology. No arbitrary schedule controls
or new world/model fields are needed.

paths: twelve day-384 anchors, twelve day-512 endpoints, twelve exact repeats,

match after accounting only for declared schedule metadata/kind/hash wrapping;
all historical dieback receipts and state remain unchanged. Validate every

Preserve all event receipts, complete lifetime/seed ledgers and all twelve

## Unchanged outcomes and decision

Reuse the exact `(384,512]` population, cohort and diversity definitions from
the [competitive-renewal protocol](landing-competitive-renewal-protocol.md).
Every 32-day birth window `(384,416]`, `(416,448]`, `(448,480]` must contain a
32-day survivor, and at least one direct parent–child pair must have both
members born in that cohort and survive 32 days. Later births remain separately
censored or observed young deaths. A killed parent can still have a posthumous
child, but cannot qualify for an age it did not survive.

Viability requires a nonempty living population throughout, at least four
living plants on average in each 16-day block, and at least four at the endpoint.
A broad within-panel signal requires all six treatments viable, at least four
passing renewal, coverage of all three seeds and both layouts, and more
renewal-passing gardens than controls. Loss of baseline species/families retained
by the paired control, or reduced variety coverage, is a tradeoff to discuss.

Publish each paired garden, primary survivors and ancestry, imposed/natural
deaths, victims' ages and deaths by age 32, seed purchases/fates and endpoint
populations. Individual adult deaths are allowed, not automatically vetoed;
young casualties and diversity losses cannot be hidden by aggregate births.
These are three weather seeds in two layouts, not six independent weather
histories. A positive result motivates fresh-condition validation, not automatic
promotion, training or deployment. Finish with a report and recommendation.
