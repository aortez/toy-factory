# Repeated local dieback and descendant renewal

Frozen before research runs. Test whether repeated spatial openings permit
plants born during the experiment to produce established children, rather than
only letting older plants' seeds refill isolated vacancies. This is an imposed
host diagnostic, not a natural mortality model, training environment or default
hazard. Follow the [competition audit](landing-seed-competition.md).

## Fixed event schedule

After the ordinary step and observations on days 192, 200, 208, ..., 304,
kill living plants whose base lies in the scheduled four-column patch. Event
number j, starting at zero, covers columns `4 * (j mod 7)` through that value
plus three. Thus the 28-column world receives two full left-to-right sweeps and
one final event at columns 0 through 3. There are fifteen scheduled events.
Record empty hits rather than choosing a different patch. All ages and lineages
are treated equally; no survival, resource or preferred-cohort targeting.

Eight days between events gives 56 days between hits at the same patch. This
is a fixed design choice, not an empirically optimized interval. The existing
320-day horizon leaves sixteen days after the last event. A failed result does
not authorize changing the schedule, patch width or endpoints in this run.

Use the existing environmental-death primitive. Retain dead bodies, stored
plant slots, spacing exclusion and normal decomposition. Discard victims'
stores without refunds; preserve seeds, soil, RNG and survivor memory. Derived
light updates normally. Death is not immediate slot release. Multi-victim and
zero-victim events must be transactional and explicitly recorded as imposed
deaths, never mislabeled starvation or physical removal. Reject combinations
with named deaths, vacancy export or paid seed replacement.

## Measurements and decision

Report all five worlds separately. Reconcile imposed and natural deaths,
seed purchases/fates, capacity, slot reclamation, offspring survival and
maintenance shortfalls. Compare chronological event exposure and victim ages;
do not hide young plants killed by a later sweep. Preserve censored survival,
posthumous children and worlds that go empty.

The primary cohort is ALL plants germinating strictly after day 192. A
qualifying renewal pair has both parent and child in that cohort and both
surviving at least sixteen days. Record one-day, sixteen-day and thirty-two-day
survival separately, including censored recent births. Count pairs and unique
reproducing parents; do not call a larger inherited generation label a new
post-onset generation. Report the deepest post-onset ancestor chain, species
coverage, endpoint living plants/seeds, empty endpoints and natural deaths by
resource cause. More refill alone is not success.

A useful pilot must increase qualifying pair counts in at least three of the
five matched worlds and leave no treatment with an empty living-plant endpoint
when its control is nonempty. This is a narrow diagnostic criterion, not
qualification of a controller or ecology. Publish harms, costs and negative
cases even if that criterion passes. The panel shares one weather seed/model
and provides no independent environmental generalization.

Finish at a report and recommendation. No broader training, device deployment,
new observation fields, fitness change or automatic promotion follows. Test
transaction rollback, boundaries, empty/multiple hits, lineage-neutral spatial
selection, cause classification, disabled parity, CLI exclusions, repeatability
and portable/raw verification before interpreting the results.
