## Interpretation limits

These four worlds share one weather seed; order variants and overlapping windows
are not independent replications. Matched birth identities need not have equal
later bodies, resources, genomes or competitors. Unmatched-cohort differences
cannot be attributed to individual-level rescue/harm.

Opening requirements are frozen-demand accounting, not alternate simulated
worlds. Removing construction or reproduction costs while retaining observed
bodies, income and competitors is intentionally nonphysical. A resource change
can change later growth, stress, reproduction, shading and soil access.

Saved traces contain aggregate soil water and leaf count/global illumination,
but not per-root cell access, per-leaf light or occluder identities. They can
show uptake deficits and light attenuation, not identify a particular competing
plant or distinguish self-shading from neighbor shading. No causal claim of
competition, misplaced seeds or a specific repair follows from these alone.

The plant-9 earlier flowering and different seed landing site were visible in
the existing portable summary before this protocol. This selection is explicitly
diagnostic and not a held-out confirmation. No reserve/bank/controller change or
policy promotion belongs to this audit.
