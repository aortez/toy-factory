# Population dawn-reserve qualification protocol

Freeze the dawn rule before testing simultaneous protection and new weather.
This is a small qualification screen, not training or firmware promotion.

## Scope and matched arms

Both arms use `--root-all-offspring`: the existing affordable birth-root priority
at actual age one, and continuous energy, afternoon and productive-daylight
checks from age two for every living non-founder, including future descendants.
Founders are excluded. No first-day expiry; saturated observation age does not
expire protection. The candidate alone conjoins the unchanged
[dawn reserve](landing-dawn-protocol.md). The birth preference remains paid,
water-checked, limited to two-root ROOT EXTEND bids, and never a resource grant.

The host-only population scope requires the entire daylight prerequisite chain,
zero focal tick/lineage, and uses GLF1 before GLE1/GLN1/GLU1/GLD1 (then GLM1 in
the candidate). Old options, identities and firmware defaults remain unchanged.
Stand receipts use the existing dawn schema, linked to the scoped run identity.

Use seasonal rainfed-crowded, no gardener/irrigation, eight plant slots,
256 shared nodes, 32 nodes per plant and eight bank slots. Pin model
`f2dc989380339614dc512f64f11f2bf03f7079c465c4c9bd18895044ec3e1567`,
from the same single-s6d697835-live champion. Keep prices, morphology, weather
rules, seed placement and all other ecology fixed. Do not tune after capture.

## Fixed panel and budget

Weather seeds: diagnostic `940cfdc4`; fresh `f7ee4528`, `04fa8f03`, `5181d0db`.
Fresh seeds are the first three outputs of `trial_seeds(0x20261002, 3)`, chosen
before outcomes. Their hexadecimal and decimal values had no matches in prior
benchmark JSON/JSON.gz files. Preserve the audited artifact list and hashes.
Fresh means unused in those saved artifacts, not a statistically untouched
global test set. The two order variants share weather and are not independent
weather replicates.

Each seed uses fixed order and the established rotating-after-day-158 order.
Each case runs daylight control and dawn candidate through day 256 (983040 ticks),
with complete stand/resource traces from tick 15 and an exact repeat. Exactly
32 native experiment calls: eight pairs × two arms × trace/repeat. Unit tests
are separate. Capture every final framebuffer, not selected winners. No extra
checkpoint searches, training, bank changes or device work.

Freeze protocol, executable, build configuration and all sources before capture.
Use a fresh no-clobber bundle; preserve failures. Exact repeats must match result
JSON, both complete traces and pixels. Independently validate scope, forecast
arithmetic, birth priorities, combined arbitration, paid prices, resource
conservation, maintenance shortages, reproduction debits/order and ledger census.
Stream full traces rather than retaining all worlds in RAM.

## Endpoints and harm checks

Report every pair, diagnostic and fresh groups separately. Record actual unpaid
maintenance events/units and payment opportunities for energy and water, for
founders and offspring separately. Use exposure-normalized rates as well as
totals; fewer bills due to early death is not an improvement. Preserve all
shortage events, per-lineage budgets, growth/flower timing, body endpoints,
daily populations, bank-only blockers and seed production.

Record complete life/seed ledgers, one-day confirmations, 16- and 32-day offspring
survival (censor younger survivors), seed-producing offspring, parents with
confirmed children, endpoint living plants/species, founder live credit and
closing-day-222..254 live credit. Compare births using ancestry, birth tick and
seed site, never a numeric ID alone after divergence. Check the common physical
prefix at the first changed choice; later candidate-state choices are component
ablations, not paired identical-world effects.

Predeclared conservative screen: no pair may lose endpoint living plants/species,
one-day confirmations, 32-day survivors, seed-producing offspring, parents with
confirmed children, founder credit or closing credit; no earlier/new empty
population; neither offspring energy nor water unpaid-unit rate may increase
(zero exposure is reported unavailable, not scored as a gain). At least one
fresh pair must strictly lower an offspring unpaid-unit rate. Any failed check
requires discussion, not an adaptive extra run or a retuned guard. Report raw
differences even when a gain elsewhere compensates in an aggregate. This screen
does not establish autonomous renewal, deployment safety or generality beyond
one model/layout/climate and four weather seeds.

Require native scope/CLI tests, host regressions, full raw verification, portable
summary/ledger verification and visual inspection of the complete paired gallery.
No default change, commit, push or PR mutation is part of this experiment.
