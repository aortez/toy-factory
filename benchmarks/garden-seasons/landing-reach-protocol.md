Read-only follow-up to landing-recruitment-trace-v1. Reuse exactly its twelve
192-day worlds, six model files, ecology, ledgers and scoring. No reselection,
training, placement intervention or firmware change. All research flags OFF.
Pin the previous recruitment results as well as its three historical inputs.

At each successful purchase, retain the parent's immutable lineage, base
column and dispersal trait, the actual landing, and all six direction/distance
outcomes of the shared production transform. Preserve aliases from reflection
at the boundary. These are support choices, not asserted exact probabilities.
Use the parent's trait, not the seed's potentially mutated genome. Capture it
before the parent can die or be reclaimed; never recover it from a later slot.
The observer consumes no random draws and does not alter the world or cache.

Retain purchase-time hypothetical mature-site masks after the final light
refresh. They are descriptive snapshots, not the actual dormant seed's check.
At every subsequent sequential seed check, retain all 28 hypothetical mature
site masks from the existing production observer. Earlier admissions within
that step may change these masks. Separate dormancy; expiry precedes checks
and supplies null sites, not fabricated zero masks. Match by parent/purchase
tick and validate the entire inventory, masks and fates against the old trace.

For each seed, freeze its purchase-time support for the whole lifetime. Retain
full mature-site-mask histograms, per-column open counts and first/last/longest
open runs. Distinguish global opportunity, reachable opportunity, alternatives
to the actual landing, and spacing-clear sites still blocked by other gates.
Spacing includes retained dead plants. Retain all overlapping blocker masks.
Report both unique columns and support-choice multiplicity. Never interpret a
later hypothetical open site in the unchanged world as a proven relocation
rescue: changing a landing could change the subsequent world.

Keep all successes and pending/censored seeds, not only failures. Highlight the
same expired-in-(62,190] cohort, tracing its whole lifetime. This retrospective
selection is not an independent performance or generalization panel.

Require exact replay JSON equality with the frozen recruitment results, tracing
disabled and repeated traced runs. Preserve exact prior recruitment trace bytes
alongside the new trace. Freeze protocol, inputs, source, binary, flags and all
artifact hashes. Independently replay the frozen bundle. Fresh outputs only;
incomplete, reordered, duplicated, corrupt or trailing records fail closed.
Test reflection/aliasing, parent versus mutated seed traits, non-mutation,
no-clobber, partial horizons, expiry/censoring and complete sample accounting.
Stop after reporting reachability and proposing a bounded placement experiment.
