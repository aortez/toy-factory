Test whether redirecting one already-affordable growth decision toward a root
changes the three observed late seedling failures. Retain two successful
seedlings as checks for harm. This is a selected-case counterfactual, not a
population policy, training run or qualification result.

## Budget and checks

Exactly 40 native experiment calls: five cases, two arms, four calls each.
For each arm capture the last ecology step before intervention (`T-15`), the
first-day checkpoint (`T+3840`), and a full day-256 trace plus exact repeat.
Detailed stand/resource traces cover `[T-15,983040]`. Ordinary unit/integration
tests are separate from this experiment budget. No exploratory sweeps or
additional native experiment calls after inspecting the result.

Before collecting, freeze source, executable, build settings, protocol and
model in a fresh no-clobber bundle. Do not edit sources during collection.
Verify common-prefix complete normalized JSON and exact framebuffer bytes;
configuration identity is the only permitted prefix difference. Control
endpoint JSON/pixels and overlapping detailed traces must match the saved
historical trajectory. Full repeats must match JSON, pixels and both traces.
Trace raw/applied priorities and the existing water forecast; old trace
formats and unconfigured run fingerprints must remain unchanged.

Check that both arms reach the same target birth and pre-growth state. Verify
one paid root allocation at the intervention, unchanged raw proposals, and
no out-of-scope priority changes. Reconcile complete first-day energy/water
budgets and record survival, shortages, morphology, reproduction and offspring
through day 256. Preserve all five outcomes, including adverse and neutral
ones, and any population effects. Later cross-arm lineage numbers are not
identities; do not match newly diverged descendants by ID. Retain censoring.

Export portable summaries, exact focal first-day receipts and images for
manual review. Include offline reanalysis and corrupt-input tests. Do not
promote this targeted, hindsight-selected intervention to a default policy.
