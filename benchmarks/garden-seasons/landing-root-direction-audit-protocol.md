# First day root direction audit protocol

Explain the early differences in the completed [wetter-root A/B](landing-root-direction.md)
using saved traces only. This is an exploratory audit after the failed population
screen, not a new experiment, qualification panel or policy change.

Pin the portable input to
`18f81abb8f76fc0a2fb4bf1e86b6b659984cadc9b2f507382187075a56b9122b`
and the raw manifest to
`814b0af751397b91db4d618ef6606dac466eb30916013ce82827e61ee4924a0e`.
Select every ecology sample from logic tick 15 through 3840 inclusive, all five
founders, all four baseline worlds and both repeats. This is the first 256
ecology steps from initialization, not a dawn-aligned day; the sun starts at
phase 64. Do not widen the window or launch a native executable.

Hash all 48 compressed stand, resource and direction streams against the frozen
input and manifest. Compare complete selected prefixes across all baseline
worlds and repeats. If they are identical within each root-direction arm, keep
one complete prefix per arm plus sixteen source/prefix checksums. Do not count
the four shared early worlds or repeats as independent evidence. A prefix
extraction is not a new verification of the later 256-day stream framing.

Reconcile uptake, overflow, maintenance, construction, reproduction and closing
stores for both resources, per founder and summed. Validate every available
growth bid and direction rewrite, every paid construction/closure, and every
reproduction visit against the original seed ledger. Preserve simultaneous seed
blockers, ready flowers, raw shoot rankings and the unchanged prices. Identify
the first resource, shoot-purchase, body and reproduction differences.

Explain the first changed root and founder 3's earlier seed using those receipts.
Separate water uptake from retained income and inventory; distinguish ability
to pay from flower availability. A different shoot choice after a root change
does not identify which neural input or memory component caused that choice.
Do not attribute later cohort losses to the first seed without additional
evidence. Missing per-root uptake or neural-state traces remain explicit limits.

Export portable complete receipts and recomputed summaries. Require repeated
exports to match, raw re-extraction and portable verification to pass, and
tests to reject missing samples, corrupt accounting, order/scope changes and
misreported conclusions. Leave firmware, native policy and all defaults alone.
