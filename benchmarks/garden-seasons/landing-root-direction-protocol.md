# Garden root direction comparison protocol

Test one change suggested by the [direction audit](landing-directions.md),
pinned to `ceb1b8a5ab527eb42b635d87b795c646a1afbed493b896917897ec7ef55dab63`.
This is a host-only paired experiment, not training or policy promotion.

Keep all four historical f7ee4528 worlds: fixed/rotating reproduction order,
with/without the dawn guard. The model, rainfed crowded scenario, seasonal
weather, day-256 endpoint, eight plant slots, 256 nodes, 32-node construction
budget and eight seed slots remain unchanged. Existing historical controls
must match their complete results, stand/resource/direction traces and pixels.

The candidate applies to every living plant's root EXTEND bids, including
founders. Only reconsider a bid that already passes the node/shoot reservation,
upfront energy/water prices, first-day water guard and applicable energy guards.
Among available endpoints that preserve that eligibility, prefer strictly
greater `min(moisture, species root uptake limit)`. Preserve the model's order
on equal potential; move the winning endpoint to the front without reordering
the others. No change when the original destination is already tied for best.
Keep action, tip, priority, recurrent memory and RNG state unchanged. Do not
make a rejected bid eligible, prioritize new soil cells, or modify shoots.

Freeze protocol, source, executable and build cache before capture. Run each
historical world with direction preference off/on, twice each: exactly sixteen
native experimental calls. Record full stand/resource/direction traces and
final pixels in both repeats. Candidate direction records add the original
model ranking so independent analysis can check every rewrite, including
non-winning bids. Give the candidate a separate run identity. With the feature
off, old trace schemas and run identities remain byte-identical.

Require exact repeats and a matching physical prefix through the first changed
purchase. Preserve first-rewrite and first-purchase witnesses; non-winning
rewrites alone must not count as a physical effect. Check equal pre-action
state, unchanged eligibility and winning tip at that boundary. Reconcile all
population resource, growth, maintenance, seed and life histories, and retain
all changed-bid receipts and every changed purchase in the portable result.
Raw verification must repeat the complete streaming analysis without simulation.

Report actual water income/overflow/spending/shortages, energy costs/shortages,
survival, reproduction, closing population credit and body allocation. Separate
founders and descendants, and match births by ancestry, tick, site and species;
numeric IDs alone are not matched after divergence. Compare the same established
population harm checks without relaxing them, and also report total seed counts.
Lower shortage counts with fewer living plants or less growth are not a rescue.
This selected single-weather panel cannot qualify a general policy even if all
pairs pass. No extra seeds, parameter sweep, retraining or second intervention
is authorized by this protocol.

Keep all native outcomes, including failures; do not rerun experiments to fix
an offline-analysis bug. Unit and short CLI tests are separate from the sixteen
experimental calls. Save the result and paired final screenshots in the repo,
record limitations and propose the next bounded question. Do not flash, change
ordinary host/device defaults, commit, push or update GitHub in this capture.
