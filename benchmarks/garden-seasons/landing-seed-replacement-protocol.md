# Paid own seed replacement experiment

Test whether a living parent can exploit newly available space by paying to
replace one of its own currently spacing-blocked seeds. This follows the
[opening opportunity audit](landing-opening-opportunities.md), not a general
recommendation to discard old seeds. Freeze this plan before observing the
new long-run outcomes. No training, firmware deployment or ordinary-default
change is included.

## Fixed inputs and panel

Use every world and configuration in the second-opening capture, SHA-256

The input opportunity audit has SHA-256
`5ea9fab6e1a8d4d5a9fc4aa90683959851d331196bb7192cbf9b47bc46d019ce`.
Preserve its five worlds, seven original focal plants and five first recruits,
including parents without banked seeds or reproductive opportunities. These
are selected histories sharing one model/weather seed, not independent
environmental replication. Earlier harm-screen failures remain unresolved.

Both arms retain the exact original model, weather, irrigation, placement,
growth/reserve rules, and named deaths at days 256 and 288 with normal
decomposition. Continue to day 320; do not retarget a death, extend the horizon
or add worlds to obtain success. The new rule activates strictly after day 288
(logic tick 1,105,920). Before then, behavior and observation rows must match.

## One replacement rule

At an ordinary reproduction visit, after germination and growth, require all
normal non-bank production gates: cadence, daylight, ready flower, cooldown,
stress, generation and protected energy/water affordability. Only a full bank
can trigger replacement. Require a free plant slot and at least four free nodes,
and at least one spacing-clear column in that parent's current dispersal support.
Spacing includes stored dead bodies and uses the unchanged three-column rule.
Do not consult light forecasts, eventual seed outcomes or preferred lineages.

Among that parent's resident seeds whose columns currently fail spacing,
select the oldest (greatest ecology age); equal ages use lower current bank
index. Other parents' seeds and clear owned seeds cannot be displaced. If
there is no candidate, do nothing. If the bank has room, use ordinary production.

Use the existing spacing-aware placement callback and its ordinary single RNG
draw; retain reflection aliases. Validate the chosen clear column before
committing anything. Replace the selected slot in place, preserving every
other bank slot's order. The fresh seed gets the ordinary mutation, age zero,
eight-tick dormancy and 32-day viability. Pay the full ordinary 48-energy and
24-water costs, consume the ready flower and set the ordinary cooldown.
No refund, free relocation, reserve bypass or capacity increase is permitted.

A denied/invalid placement must not consume purchase RNG, lose the old seed
or debit resources. Existing enclosing step rollback contracts still apply.
The new seed is a new purchase; the old seed's terminal outcome is explicitly
`replaced`, not expired or germinated. Retain exact old identity/age/column and
new landing receipts. Observer enablement cannot affect the transaction.

## Fixed comparison and evidence

Run exactly twenty full native calls after focused transaction/CLI tests:
five controls (both deaths, replacement off), five treatments, five exact
treatment repeats and five untraced treatments. Controls must reproduce the
archived day-320 second-opening treatment results, frames and four traces.
Treatment repeats match states, frames and all traces; untraced treatments
match final state/result/frame. No parameter sweep or automatic retry/resume.

Freeze source/build/model/protocol identities in a fresh output bundle before
capture. Keep existing audits unchanged. Retain complete stand, adult-resource,
direction and reach traces plus a separately framed replacement trace covering
every ecology step, including zeros. Ledger production visits keep their
ordinary bank blocker: a paid replacement at bank-full is an explicit
exception evidenced by its own receipt, never silently reclassified as vacancy.

Reconcile seed inventory, new purchases and all terminal fates; exact costs;
ordered reproduction; matched plants' maintenance shortfalls and natural
deaths; and the old seeds' outcomes in unchanged controls where identities
match. Record first effect and validate the entire common prefix. Include
paired endpoint images and portable results, with repeat/observer checks.
Do not infer extra children from overlap counts or from screenshot appearance.

## Outcomes and stopping point

Report every followed parent's seed purchases, replacements, children,
first-day/16-day/32-day survival (right-censor when the cutoff is too early),
and living descendants. Separate original focal, first recruit and later
cohorts. Also retain whole-world births/deaths, pools and species outcomes.

A useful selected-panel result needs at least one first recruit to gain a
child surviving 16 days, without extra natural deaths or maintenance
shortfalls among matched pre-event survivors and without reducing established
offspring elsewhere in the panel. More purchases alone is not success.
Any regression stays visible; this screen alone cannot promote the rule.

If implementation or evidence checks fail, stop the capture and preserve the
failed bundle rather than change the rule around observed outcomes. Fix and
validate the cause before proposing a new explicitly labeled capture. Discuss
the findings before another ecological intervention or broader training.
