## Reproduction accounting

Retain every selected plant's reproduction visit, including non-cadence,
cooldown, full-bank, stress, light, generation, energy, water and flower
refusals, and all simultaneous blockers. Include the other plants' visits,
their traversal order and bank occupancy so that seed-bank arbitration is
checkable against the original seed ledger. Reconcile target stores and

Keep every seed purchase in the common window, not only the first extra one.
Report both arms' gates at every tick when either buys a seed, plus eventual
seed outcomes and one-day offspring survival where observable.

## Water timing and capacity resets

For every seed purchase, find the first later sample at which the actual
plant's post-uptake water reaches its 512-unit capacity. Also record the first
post-step return to at least its pre-purchase water store. A capacity refill
means the purchase does not remain as an unfilled inventory gap along the
observed trajectory; it does not erase changes to offspring or the world.
Report missing refills explicitly rather than assuming replenishment.

For each pair, locate the earliest unpaid water maintenance in the common
window. Then find the last preceding ecology sample at which **both** arms
have 512 water immediately after uptake. Report whether seed spending occurs
after this shared saturation. Starting at that exact stage, balance all
remaining income, overflow, growth, reproduction, paid upkeep, discard and
closing stores through the common endpoint. Exclude the anchor's already
completed uptake but include its later maintenance and spending. If no shared
saturation exists, retain that absence instead of choosing another convenient
anchor. Also retain the last positive water-income sample and the first
shortage in each arm, with full window balances and equal exposure.

This is accounting on actual histories, not a frozen-income skip-seed replay
or a claim that blocking reproduction would preserve the same environment.
Distinguish a persistent seed debit from later loss of retained water. Stop
after explaining the evidence and propose any next causal test separately.
