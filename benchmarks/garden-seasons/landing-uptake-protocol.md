## Frozen capture and neutrality checks

For each of fixed/rotating control/candidate, merge the saved offline audit's
selected intervals across plants and record uptake throughout that union. This
includes all shortages, preceding-day context, founders and common plant 9,
not selected successful or striking examples. Each world runs once with uptake
enabled and once disabled: exactly eight native experiment calls. Retain full
stand/resource traces, complete result ledgers and final pixels for both calls.
Require byte-identical stand/resource traces and pixels plus equal result JSON,
both between enabled/disabled runs and against the historical population capture.
No extra checkpoint search, training, policy tuning, firmware or GitHub changes.

Freeze this protocol, native executable, build configuration and sources before
the eight calls. Preserve failures in a fresh no-clobber bundle. Native unit/CLI
tests are separate from these experimental calls. Run host regressions and
explicit observer rollback, boundary and transfer-accounting tests.

## Attribution and interpretation

Independently reconstruct the saturated opacity grid and the original rounded
ray walk. Every sampled leaf and the full light grid must match. Reconcile leaf
energy and root water against existing unsaturated resource receipts, including
overflow and plants dying or being reclaimed later in the same step. Replay the
recorded root order against before-soil and require the exact after-soil grid.

For a receiving leaf, distinguish its own plant, other live plants and dead
plants as shade sources. Compute each class's removal effect on that same
recorded geometry, with cell saturation, ambient floor and integer energy
quantization preserved. These removal effects overlap and must not be summed
as independent shares. They are instantaneous diagnostics, not alternate worlds
or evidence that removing a class is a safe intervention.

For roots, separate moisture absent before uptake from depletion by earlier
roots in the same step; identify earlier own-plant versus other-plant consumers.
Record storage overflow separately from uptake. Do not call earlier consumption
recoverable water or infer that reordering roots improves long-term outcomes.
Rain, transport and previous-step use are not individually attributed by the
pre-uptake snapshot.

Report both arms/orders, all selected failure windows and aggregate checks.
Retain per-plant step accounting and representative raw shortage receipts in
portable evidence; retain all raw observer traces for complete reconstruction.
Use the results to choose the next question, not to tune another reserve during
this capture. Matched IDs and similar morphology across order variants are not
proof of equal intervening environments. Survival remains censored at day 256.
