## Outcomes

spacing opportunities and observed usable/reachable site opportunities. Partition
occupancy by season, drought and cold without treating coincidence as proof of
an environmental cause. Distinguish living full pools from decomposing occupants.

For every post-onset slot-release event, derive newly spacing-clear columns
against the immediately prior stored census. Group simultaneous releases rather
than attributing shared columns to individual corpses. Follow each new column

and reproduction from the existing ledger. Do not count multiple columns closed

recorded sequences, not a causal intervention removing one plant. An unobserved
site between checks stays unobserved, and a quiet interval is not permanent
