## Interpretation

Assess each finalist separately. The primary durability criterion is a strictly
higher mean late-period renewal score than fixed wear, not occupancy or a
survivor-only average. Report all three periods, per-seed/layout comparisons,
closing-window participation, renewal in every late window, endpoint living,
dormant and extinct families, and new extinctions after day 608. Dormant means
no living plant but at least one pending seed; extinction means neither remains.
Report raw and equally condition-weighted extinction, plus paired gains/losses.

Neither a pass nor a failure changes the objective, selects between finalists,
promotes firmware or authorizes another search. Stronger late renewal can still
come with a survival cost. A finite matched follow-up does not establish
indefinite stability or behavior when every competing family uses the same model.
