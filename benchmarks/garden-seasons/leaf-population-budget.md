# Missed seed openings are not an immediate repair-spending problem

Skipping the repair payment on the opening step would make none of shrub 17's
eight missed open-bank visits affordable, so a reproductive-reserve guard is not
the next fix. Five misses follow a full energy store with no later repair
spending; the other three fall in low-income episodes, energy around day 191 and
water around day 199.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-budget-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_budget.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory and
  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.

  `ab314a17361fe939525f61c6d340fc45d9a0058f0fb0fdc9c770f432709728ed`.
