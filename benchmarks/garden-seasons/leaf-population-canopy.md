# Dawn exposure combines boundary access and worn foliage

The original shrub's dawn rays meet only its own foliage before leaving the left
boundary or reaching the ambient floor; the relocated shrub's also meet founder
flower 1. Shade is not the whole lower-canopy problem: with all shading omitted
at the recorded leaf conditions, the relocated lower leaves would contribute
only 22.33 energy, against the original lower canopy's observed 82.75.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-canopy-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_canopy.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory and
  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
- Result, kept locally: `leaf-population-canopy-results.json.gz`, SHA256
  `bffc7454ca81b41470461c493b6a8a2260072fe485aab4dadefffc99e24171e1`.
