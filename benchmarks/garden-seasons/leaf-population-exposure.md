# The lower canopy accounts for the dawn energy gap

Both shrubs' upper leaves contribute almost the same energy during the focal
dawn; the gap is in the lower canopy, about 83 energy in shrub 19 against 7 in
shrub 17. Of shrub 17's 15 lower leaves, eight never reach a productive light
band, five receive productive light but contribute no energy, and two contribute
energy.

  figure.
