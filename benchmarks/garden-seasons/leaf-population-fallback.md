# Fewer blockers help one repair variant and hurt two

Preferring the destination with the fewest current spacing blockers rescues the
shrub family when both repair gates are restored on weather seed `9a903368`, but
reduces renewal under the light-only and condition-only variants. It is a
controlled placement experiment, not a general replacement for the existing
fallback.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-fallback-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_fallback.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory and
  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
- Result, kept locally: `leaf-population-fallback-results.json.gz`, SHA256
  `61381e2a5d72106e54212c78715718b1aef289bb91375518db204e6dfe5418ce`.
- Full report in [PR #38](https://github.com/aortez/toy-factory/blob/44b12803cf4f7b966a003bc0857b1bb061fd3374/benchmarks/garden-seasons/leaf-population-fallback.md).
