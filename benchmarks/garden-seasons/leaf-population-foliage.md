# Dawn exposure explains this missed opening

During the focal dawn, original-placement shrub 19 collects 159 energy and
fewer-blocker shrub 17 collects 84. Shrub 19 has 1.84 times the summed usable
light bands, while light-weighted leaf condition is almost the same, 46.5%
against 45.8%. Neither plant repairs or grows in this interval.
