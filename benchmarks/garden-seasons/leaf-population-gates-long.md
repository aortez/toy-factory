# Restoring both repair gates enables later shrub generations

Through day 608, restoring only the light gate keeps the founder alive and
eventually yields one established child. Restoring both gates yields six new
shrubs, including two qualifying later-generation offspring, but coincides with
the loss of the ground-cover family.

  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
