# Repair from day zero: light-only leads, but timing matters

With gates restored from day zero on a four-seed panel, light-only gives the
strongest renewal, including one family with qualifying children in every
scoring window. Two shrub families still go extinct, and restoring both gates
does worse than either gate alone, so the late-rescue result does not generalize
uniformly to early growth.
