# Light gate restoration preserves shrubs on a second weather seed

On a second weather seed, restoring the light gate again keeps the shrub family
alive through day 608, and restoring the condition gate alone does not. The
survival result repeats; the renewal ranking of light-only against both-restored
does not.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-gates-transfer-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_gates_transfer.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory
  and `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
- Result, kept locally: `leaf-population-gates-transfer-results.json.gz`, SHA256
  `4c2a1c1522ec0ad696a9214f8b40941f14e3f698d9f5973aed7883b11d5ee175`.
