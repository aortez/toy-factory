# Restoring the light gate preserves the shrub through winter

Restoring Search 1's light gate to the fixed-wear gate prevents the selected
founder's death through day 256; restoring its condition gate alone does not. No
arm produces new shrub offspring after the intervention, so this is evidence for
survival, not yet for renewal.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-gates-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_gates.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory and
  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
- Result, kept locally: `leaf-population-gates-results.json.gz`, SHA256
  `a73a6f20cf420348fe1684ef73ea0ea081e872586c91287a4c7940064d9147a1`.
