# Family losses under uniform repair policies

Search 1's higher renewal comes with the loss of every shrub family in the
tested gardens. Six of its eight extra losses against fixed wear come after
descendants have reached adulthood, so this is not only a failure to establish
the first generation.

- Script: `population_losses.py` derives this offline from local archives;
  `--out` recomputes and `--check` compares.

  `a0c7ddbf6d5967efcb9622a7c24fa57f8e8729f3504902df3b3a3e91a019bca8`.
