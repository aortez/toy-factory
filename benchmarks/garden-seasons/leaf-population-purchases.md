# Seed purchases choose among blocked destinations

Seven of eight shrub-family seed purchases in days `[30,35)` have no
spacing-clear reachable column, so the contrasting handoffs do not come from
overlooking a free destination. In those cases placement keeps its original
random fallback, and the daylight ranking cannot distinguish the blocked
alternatives.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-purchases-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_purchases.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory and
  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
- Result, kept locally: `leaf-population-purchases-results.json.gz`, SHA256
  `2d94fda91c831ac484c906588d0c9db5f0400ee031093c7a2aff9ba532b2f24d`.
- Full report in [PR #38](https://github.com/aortez/toy-factory/blob/44b12803cf4f7b966a003bc0857b1bb061fd3374/benchmarks/garden-seasons/leaf-population-purchases.md).
