# Winter repair decisions on identical observations

Given identical leaf observations, the learned repair controllers do choose
differently from fixed wear. In the selected Search 1 shrub most differences
come before winter, while energy and water are plentiful, so the final decline
is not explained by an inability to afford those earlier repairs.
