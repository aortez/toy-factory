# Repair light threshold: no setting gives renewal and variety together

Twelve settings of the shared repair light gate ran on sixteen fresh weather
seeds in both layouts. None gives Search 1's late renewal together with fixed
wear's founder-family survival, and the earlier tradeoff reproduces: Search 1
renews 0.133 against fixed wear's 0.014 and loses 72 of 128 founder families
against 42. Settings that keep fixed wear's variety renew at most 0.029; the
only one that out-renews Search 1 loses 93 families.

Which species survives depends on the rule, so the
[roadmap](../../docs/garden-alife-roadmap.md) turns to coexistence mechanisms
before any training.
