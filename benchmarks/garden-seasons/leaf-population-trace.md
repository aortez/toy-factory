# Winter energy loss and blocked shrub recruitment

The first extra Search 1 shrub-family loss has two bottlenecks. Its last plants
run out of winter energy as leaf condition falls, and its two remaining seeds
never pass the spacing check before expiring. Longer seed life or less repair
spending is not yet a supported fix.

- [Protocol](leaf-population-trace-protocol.md).
- Script: `population_trace.py`; `run` collects into a fresh directory and
  `verify` checks an unpacked archive.
- Result, kept locally: `leaf-population-trace-results.json.gz`, SHA256
  `b85e6ac58ee50462a4c025a1c3adb09c496b3f1ac7aa569b27d719737eb40178`.
- Full report in [PR #38](https://github.com/aortez/toy-factory/blob/44b12803cf4f7b966a003bc0857b1bb061fd3374/benchmarks/garden-seasons/leaf-population-trace.md).
