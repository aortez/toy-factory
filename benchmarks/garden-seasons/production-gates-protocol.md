# Exact seed-production gate observation

Freeze this protocol before collecting gate outcomes. Replay all 32 matched
worlds (both arms, 64 trajectories) from the fresh first-day reserve confirmation
through day 128. Keep its eight weather seeds, both layouts and both controllers;
no selection by attractiveness or outcome. Keep the wide-dispersal research build
and unchanged first-day energy/water guard. No ecology, controller, fitness,
training, device or default changes are part of this investigation.

Observe each plant at its actual sequential reproduction visit, after growth,
dawn spent-flower clearing and cooldown decrement, before reproduction debits.
Record stored/required energy and water, cooldown, bank occupancy, stress,
sun strength, immature/spent/ready flower counts, selected flower, all ordinary
gate flags and actual production. Use the same reserve calculation in the
decision and observer. A full bank can result from an earlier plant's purchase
on the same step. Keep first-failed-check counts separate from overlapping
limits. Neither is a counterfactual rescue estimate.

Compare each ecology state hash, creation total and ordered newly created seed
parents with the saved primary inspector trace. Check every observed lineage
against the frozen birth/death history. Match every seed production to the
previous audit's creation ledger. Run the collector twice and require identical
output. Keep native source/build hashes before and after collection. A changed
trajectory, incomplete panel, missing visit, unaccounted production or invalid
receipt fails the audit. Unit tests must also compare entire world bytes between
ordinary and observed steps and exercise equality/one-past gate boundaries.

Primary analysis: previously established descendants that produced no seeds,
whole-history and closing-year cohorts separately, including dead parents.
Use living maintenance-cadence visits for timing denominators; also show the
narrower daylight, cooldown-clear, mature/unspent-flower window. Report plants
with no such window, first-failed gates and overlapping limits within it,
resource shortfalls and bank occupancy. Retain each parent's counts and split
whole/closing results by arm, weather, layout, controller and species. Show
seed-producing parents as a comparator, without treating different resulting
cohorts as individual causal matches. Identify follow-up duration and do not
call a young/pending parent permanently infertile.

Retain portable parent aggregates with trace/source provenance and a written
report. Raw visit receipts remain in ignored artifacts. Recompute retained
aggregates in CI, reject corrupt inputs, and reuse the prior verified images
because replay state must be unchanged. Choose any rule change only after
discussing the measured bottleneck; do not tune during this observer pass.
