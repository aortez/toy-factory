## Seed residence and ownership

Each seed is identified by world, arm, parent lineage and creation tick. Families
are founder ancestry within a world, not globally interchangeable numeric IDs.
Record every family's seed counts, species, target columns and bank occupation,
including families that produce no seeds. Do not match post-divergence descendant
IDs between arms. Retain partitions by weather block, layout, controller and
species rather than interpreting 32 worlds as independent weather replicates.

Count post-step seed samples from creation (inclusive) to removal (exclusive),
or through day 128 for pending seeds. Report whole-history and closing-calendar
windows (strictly after day 112), reconciling exactly to the capacity study's
occupancy totals. Also partition residence into seed ages under one day, one to
four days, four to sixteen days and sixteen to thirty-two days. No expiry sample
is counted: expiry is at exactly thirty-two days. Report residence after a
parent's death separately; those seeds can still germinate.

Partition occupied samples by eventual observed seed/child outcome: expiry,
pending seed, young living/dead child without complete day follow-up, early
child death, day-surviving child without durable reproduction, and a durable
child (it survives a day and has its own day-surviving child). These categories
use the fixed day-128 horizon and are descriptive, not information available to
a seed admission policy. Keep censoring explicit; pending is not failed.

Report resolved germination waits and censored pending ages separately. Sum
mature post-step blocker masks, preserving overlap. They describe observed site
states, not actual germination attempts or counterfactual rescue counts. Derive
closing masks from the original raw records; do not proportionally divide whole-
history masks. Reconcile their denominators to exact residence intervals.

For each world/window report the largest family's cumulative occupation share,
retaining the full family table. Count shares of at least 75% and exactly 100%;
neither is a success criterion or a policy threshold.

## Matched incumbent accounting

Use all 132 incumbents alive at first capacity divergence, with the previously
classified 57 earlier, 21 later and 54 unchanged death outcomes. Never replace
this census with conveniently selected examples. For each individual compare
identical calendar windows in both arms:

- From the first capacity divergence through the first death in either arm,
  inclusive; if neither dies, stop at day 128.
- The final Garden day of that same window, clipped to its start.

The first window includes the extra seed purchase at divergence and starts from
the preceding shared stores. Include terminal income/upkeep/discard from the
exact death receipt, rather than treating cleared resources as maintenance.
Reconcile initial stores plus income minus overflow, upkeep, growth, seed costs
and terminal discard to final stores for both resources. Reconcile seed debits
to the independently retained seed ledger. Preserve start/end/last-live states,
shortage onset, receipt and stage totals, including zero or reduced seed spending.

Summarize paired spending/income/upkeep changes for earlier/later/unchanged groups
and all incumbents. These comparisons can reject a universal extra-spending
explanation but cannot isolate seed spending from changed shading, competition,
growth or uptake. Do not present pooled raw death counts as mortality rates.
