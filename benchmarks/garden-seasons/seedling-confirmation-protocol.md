# Fresh eight-weather-seed confirmation of first-day reserves

Freeze this protocol before collecting fresh outcomes. Confirm the unchanged
[all-offspring candidate](seedling-population.md), not a revised controller.
This is a bounded host experiment; it does not authorize promotion, training,
device flashing, or qualification of the ecology.

## Fixed candidate and panel

Both arms use the exact inspector and replayer binaries, Garden build flags and
C sources from the all-offspring screen. Only the analysis/render helper gains
explicit panel/filename parameters; recomputing the old evidence must produce
its unchanged gates, metrics and summary. CMake only registers the new tests.

Control is the original baseline/adaptive controller. Treatment uses the existing
`--seedling-reserve all`: combined energy/water reserves during nonfounder ages
1..256 ecology samples, with the same wet-root exception and night veto. Neither
age 257 onward nor founders are guarded. No thresholds, growth/seed costs,
candidate order, arbitration, memory, weather, resources, gardener, irrigation,
capacity, model or fitness function changes.

Use seed base `0x73656135`, with the existing deterministic sequence
`(base XOR ((i + 1) * 0x9e3779b9)) & 0xffffffff`, indices zero through seven:

`ed52188c`, `4f0b9247`, `a9c30c1e`, `0bb887d1`, `647001a8`, `c629bb63`,
`20e1353a`, `82deacfd`.

Check that these do not overlap the prior seasonal recruitment/scattering/reserve
panels. Cross them with rainfed/rainfed-crowded and baseline/adaptive: 32 paired
worlds. These are eight fresh weather blocks, not 32 independent weather
replicates. Both arms keep the same wider scattering, seasonal climate,
256-node/eight-plant/eight-seed ecology and 128-day horizon (491,520 logic ticks).
Normal host/device scattering stays narrow and the reserve adapter stays off.

## Unchanged evaluation and stopping rule

Reuse all nine aggregate gates from the
[known-seed protocol](seedling-population-protocol.md): retain closing birth
worlds, full-day survivors and durable parents; retain historical durable parents
and multi-viable-species endpoints; strictly improve at least one of those three
closing renewal metrics; retain the eligible closing survival fraction; introduce
no empty living-plus-seed world and no zero-living endpoint.

Use exact fraction cross-products, the same strictly-after-day-112 birth window,
full-day eligibility, boundary deaths and censored young births. A durable parent
and at least one of its germinated children must each survive a full day. Keep
full-history and closing cohorts, two-day follow-up, resource/weather categories
of early deaths, offspring-free survivors, founder turnover, seeds created/
expired and living versus seed-bank diversity distinct.

Report per-world increases/ties/decreases, all adverse pairs and partitions by
weather, layout and controller. Do not pool these fresh worlds with the earlier
screen to rescue a failed gate. An aggregate pass is evidence for this candidate,
not proof of universal improvement, sustainable diversity, or a qualified training
environment. Stop after all 32 pairs whether positive, mixed or negative. Do not
retune, replace seeds, extend only favorable runs or start training.

and all frozen dependencies except the two declared orchestration/test-registry
